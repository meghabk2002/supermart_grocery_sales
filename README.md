🔍 Key Insights – Supermart Grocery Sales
Top Segments: Consumer segment drives the most sales and profits.

Best Categories: Office Supplies lead in sales, but Technology is more profitable.

Regional Trends: West and East outperform; Central struggles with profitability.

State Highlights: California and New York dominate; Texas has high sales but low profits.

Profitability Issues: Sub-categories like Tables show consistent losses.

Customer Behavior: Consumers are the most profitable; Home Office is least.

Order Quantity: Larger orders don’t always mean higher profits.

Shipping Modes: Standard Class is most common; Same Day is rare and costlier.
